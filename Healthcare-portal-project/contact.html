FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about HealthCare+.

How do I book an appointment?

Go to the Doctors page, browse or search for a specialist, and click "Book Appointment." You'll be taken to the booking form where you can choose a date and time slot. The whole process takes under 2 minutes.

Are the doctors on HealthCare+ verified?

Yes. Every doctor listed on HealthCare+ undergoes a thorough verification process including medical license validation, credential checks, hospital affiliation verification, and background screening before being listed on our platform.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before your scheduled appointment. Contact our support team at +91 12345 67890 or email support@healthcareplus.in.

Is my personal health data safe?

Absolutely. We follow HIPAA-compliant data security practices. All your personal and health information is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is never sold or shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), any previous medical reports, prescriptions, or test results. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork.

Are online consultations available?

We're actively working on adding telemedicine / video consultation capabilities. Stay tuned for updates! For now, all appointments are in-person at the doctor's listed hospital or clinic.
