Our Story

Born From a Real Need

In 2020, our founder experienced firsthand the frustration of finding the right specialist quickly. Long waiting times, confusing referral chains, and lack of transparent information inspired the creation of HealthCare+.

We started with just 5 doctors and a handful of patients. Today, we've grown into a trusted platform connecting hundreds of patients with 50+ verified specialists across multiple cities.

  • All doctors verified with license checks
  • Real patient reviews and ratings
  • Transparent fees — no hidden charges
  • HIPAA-compliant data protection
  • 24/7 patient support helpline
